# ADR-0001 — Dedicated Atlas Repository

## Status
Accepted for bootstrap.

## Decision
Maintain *A Mathematical Atlas of Adaptive Intelligence* in a dedicated public repository, `grandchallenge/ADAPTIVE-INTELLIGENCE-ATLAS`.

## Rationale
The Atlas crosses mathematics, machine intelligence, numerical analysis, systems, agents, memory, and governance. Housing it inside MATH-PROGRAMME would misstate its institutional scope. The repository remains downstream of shared GCL standards and may consume research/certification evidence without becoming a certification authority.

## Consequences
Atlas-specific doctrine stays local. Reusable cross-programme machinery may later be proposed to `gcl-standards` through its normal process.

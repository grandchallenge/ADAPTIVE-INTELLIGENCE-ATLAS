# ADR-0003 — Wolfram and Computational Figure Provenance

## Status
Accepted for bootstrap.

## Decision
Every computational Atlas figure retains its generator source, relevant environment/version, parameters, precision where material, representation class, literal semantics, nonliteral semantics, and support role.

## Rationale
A rendered figure is part of the argument but not automatically evidence or proof. Reconstructability prevents decorative authority from replacing mathematical support.

## Consequences
The Figure Register and per-figure manifests are required before a computational figure is considered publication-ready.

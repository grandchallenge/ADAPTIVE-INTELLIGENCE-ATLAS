# ADR-0005 — Six-Keystone Composition Grammar

## Status

Accepted on merge of SYNTHESIS-001.

## Context

The architecture bootstrap defined editorial doctrine before substantial manuscript material existed. Six keystone chapters have now been drafted and independently audited:

- Geometry;
- Non-normality;
- Attention as an Operator;
- Optimizer-State Dynamics;
- Boundary Contracts;
- Replayable Evidence Objects.

Those chapters provide empirical evidence about which authoring practices survive across mathematical, computational, compositional, and documentary material.

## Decision

Promote the recurring practices demonstrated by the six keystones into durable Atlas-local protocol:

1. `governance/CHAPTER_COMPOSITION_PROTOCOL.md`;
2. `governance/EPISTEMIC_STATUS.yaml`;
3. `governance/COMPUTATIONAL_WITNESS_STANDARD.md`;
4. `governance/SOURCE_LOCK_STANDARD.md`;
5. `governance/CHAPTER_FAMILY_ROLLOUT.md`.

The protocol is functional rather than templatic. Chapters need not share literal headings or equal amounts of mathematics.

## Frozen distinctions

The Atlas must preserve these distinctions unless a later ADR explicitly changes them:

- ambient coordinate space versus admissible state space;
- score/representation versus operator action;
- asymptotic stability versus finite-horizon amplification;
- local mechanism versus empirical prevalence;
- local contract versus global safety;
- replay versus truth, formal verification, independent replication, certification, and authority;
- public project evidence versus programme context;
- literal figure semantics versus schematic/metaphorical presentation.

## Consequence

Future chapter-family drafting inherits a tested grammar rather than six independent local conventions.

The six keystones remain `draft-v0.1`; synthesis of editorial grammar does not promote their mathematical or institutional status.

## Supersession rule

Changes to the canonical composition grammar, epistemic labels, source-lock doctrine, or computational-witness doctrine require an ADR.

Ordinary chapter-specific variation does not.

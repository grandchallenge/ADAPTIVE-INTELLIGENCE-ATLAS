## Open-obligation discipline

Geometry-derived optimization must show a declared advantage tied to actual matrix, manifold, conditioning, or variational structure.

Operator-valued position must establish structure beyond ordinary RoPE block algebra before downstream operator claims are promoted.

Adaptive depth must connect stopping or compute allocation to a declared error or task signal and compare against matched fixed-depth controls.

Shared memory must specify what leaves weights, what remains parametric, and which provenance, retrieval, or transaction guarantees are necessary.

Learning-progress search must separate local gain from long-horizon curriculum quality and control confounds.

Minimal curricula must define the mechanism class, reconstruction procedure, resource budget, and target capability family.

The Residual retains open existence, uniqueness, dimension, learnability, and operational-reconstruction questions outside the audited finite formalization.

Benign nonconvexity requires explicit landscape conditions and optimizer dynamics; successful training alone is not sufficient.

Boundary contracts must establish sufficiency of exposed interface variables and cannot infer global safety from local compatibility.

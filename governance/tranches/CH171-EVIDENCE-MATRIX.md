## Programme matrix

### 1. Geometry-derived optimization

Current draft substrate at the protected snapshot:
- SECOND-001: draft-v0.1.

Current architecture-stage programmes:
- MATRIXOPT-001.
- MANOPT-001.
- VARIOPT-001.

Canonical contracts:
- MATRIXOPT studies Shampoo, polar factors, orthogonalized updates, Muon-like methods, and square versus rectangular geometry.
- MANOPT develops tangent gradients, retractions, constrained motion, and sphere/Stiefel optimization.
- VARIOPT develops divergences as geometry, discrete Lagrangians, symplectic updates, and MODULUS-style derivation of update rules.

Smallest open obligation:
For one declared problem family, identify a structure-aware update rule and show exactly which matrix, manifold, conditioning, or variational property changes the predicted or measured optimization behavior relative to matched first-order controls.

Disallowed promotion:
Naming a method "geometric" or observing one successful run does not establish geometry-derived advantage.

### 2. Operator-valued position

Current draft substrate at the protected snapshot:
- POSGEOM-001: draft-v0.1.

Current architecture-stage programme:
- RPO-001.

Canonical contract:
Move from positional vectors to low-dimensional operators, frequency modes, DC components, and head-specific specialization.

Smallest open obligation:
Show in one declared model/head/position setting that a compact relative-position operator captures reproducible structure not exhausted by ordinary RoPE block identities, and separate frequency/DC/head-specialization claims.

Disallowed promotion:
Ordinary RoPE relative-phase algebra is not itself an RPO theorem.

### 3. Adaptive depth as error control

Current draft substrate at the protected snapshot:
- DEPTH-001: draft-v0.1.
- NETNUM-001: draft-v0.1.

Current architecture-stage programme:
- ADAPTDEPTH-001.

Canonical contract:
Connect learned stopping and adaptive compute to local error estimation and adaptive time stepping.

Smallest open obligation:
Define a measurable error or task-quality surrogate, show that the stopping/depth controller tracks it, and compare against fixed-depth controls at matched compute or matched quality.

Disallowed promotion:
Variable depth alone is not evidence of numerical error control.

### 4. Persistent and shared memory

Current draft substrate at the protected snapshot:
- EXTMEM-001: draft-v0.1.

Canonical contract:
Test which knowledge should leave parameters and enter persistent shared memory.

Smallest open obligation:
For a declared workload, partition information among weights, context, and persistent memory, then measure the effects of provenance, retrieval indexing, update semantics, and shared access.

Disallowed promotion:
The external-memory thesis does not imply that all useful knowledge should leave model parameters.

### 5. Learning progress as search

Current draft substrate at the protected snapshot:
- CURRICULUM-001: draft-v0.1.

Current architecture-stage programme:
- PROGRESSSEARCH-001.

Canonical contract:
Treat learning progress and declared generalization-state evidence as feedback for choosing the next experience and searching experience space.

Smallest open obligation:
Compare a progress-driven selector against state-matched baselines over a horizon long enough to expose greedy-myopia failures, controlling for dose, difficulty, and compute.

Disallowed promotion:
Positive immediate learning progress is not proof of long-horizon curriculum optimality.

### 6. Minimal curricula and reasoning bases

Current architecture-stage programme:
- MINCURR-001.

Inherited audited substrate:
- RESIDUAL-001: draft-v0.1.

Canonical contract:
Ask for the smallest early mechanisms and reasoning operations from which broad later capability can be reconstructed, distinguishing acquisition, persistence, accessibility, and behavioural expression.

Smallest open obligation:
Declare a capability family, mechanism vocabulary, resource budget, and reconstruction procedure; then remove or substitute candidate early mechanisms to find a minimal sufficient set under that declaration.

Disallowed promotion:
A short curriculum or small example set is not automatically a minimal reasoning basis.

### 7. The Residual

Current draft substrate at the protected snapshot:
- RESIDUAL-001: draft-v0.1.

Established inside the Atlas formalization:

Still open outside bounded settings:

## Governance rule for advancement

A frontier item advances only when new evidence is:
- bound to the exact target;
- typed as proof, computation, observation, or bounded experiment;
- compared against the relevant control;
- checked against the source/authority boundary;
- replayed after identity-changing repair.

Execution of a research task is not promotion of its strongest interpretation.

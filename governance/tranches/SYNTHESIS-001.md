## Canonical outputs

### Chapter composition

`governance/CHAPTER_COMPOSITION_PROTOCOL.md`

Freezes the functional sequence:

1. opening problem;
2. bounded pedagogical device where useful;
3. formal/documentary object;
4. exact derivation or reconstruction;
5. computational witness where material;
6. figure semantics;
7. counterexamples/failure boundaries;
8. downstream handoff;
9. source lock/provenance;
10. epistemic status/promotion boundary.

The protocol is functional, not a mandatory literal heading template.

### Source locking

`governance/SOURCE_LOCK_STANDARD.md`

Freezes:

- source identity;
- authority/scope;
- immutable revision/content identity where appropriate;
- public GCL evidence versus programme context;
- mutable-source treatment;
- negative source findings;
- no citation laundering.

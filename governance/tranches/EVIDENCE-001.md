## Central object

and documentary support notation

The support arrow is not logical entailment.

## Frozen distinctions

claim ≠ support.

support ≠ interpretation.

exact computation ≠ universal proof.

observation ≠ mechanism.

source identity ≠ source authority.

public project evidence ≠ programme context.

institutional status ≠ mathematical truth.

## No scalar hierarchy

The eleven canonical epistemic classes are treated as typed roles rather than rungs of one evidence ladder.

This is required because Definition, Open Problem, Interpretation, and Institutional Status describe semantically different axes rather than weaker or stronger versions of one proposition.

## Computational witness

ATLAS-CW-EVIDENCE-001 contrasts:

- an exact exhaustive check over integers -10 through 10;
- the corresponding universal parity theorem.

The finite witness proves the finite claim.

The universal claim is established by a separate elementary parity proof.

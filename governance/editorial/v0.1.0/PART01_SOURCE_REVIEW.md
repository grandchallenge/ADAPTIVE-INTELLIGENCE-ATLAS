## THESIS — ATLAS-CH-THESIS-001

**Strength:** the system-over-model thesis is deliberately labeled a project synthesis rather than a theorem, and the seven-role system tuple is presented as a provisional map legend, not a universal ontology. A good initial argument exists.

**ER-P1-01 (section 8):** references to drafting the six keystones first and the present foundation tranche backfilling the reader path are obsolete production-process narration in a published book. Relocate that history to an editorial note or remove it from the reader's structural argument.

**ER-P1-02 (sections 3, 7, 9):** the ten conceptual shifts are useful, but the normative versus descriptive distinction and the list of what later chapters must earn substantially restate the opening thesis. Refactor these into a short motivated table and a falsifiability/limits section.

**Proposed sequence:** Why a system boundary; the seven explanatory roles; a small set of recurring transitions; what the thesis does NOT prove and how it can fail; the reader's path into MAP and OBJECTS.

**Disposition:** REVISION REQUIRED (narrative/production language). No mathematical claim strengthening authorized.

## MAP — ATLAS-CH-MAP-001

**Strength:** navigation versus dependency authority and stable IDs versus changing chapter order are lucid. The six alternative reading routes and concrete protocol are useful to readers.

**ER-P1-03 (sections 9–27):** the epistemic vocabulary, figure classes, source-locks, replay and audit discussions consume the middle of a chapter billed as navigation. EVIDENCE repeats much of that teaching in greater depth. Keep a compact legend here and direct the reader to EVIDENCE rather than teaching the full machinery twice.

**ER-P1-04 (sections 28–36):** the most actionable reader routes and navigation table appear at the end. Promote them nearer the beginning, before the glossary-level exposition.

**ER-P1-05 (math presentation):** the chapter's bracket-delimited TeX expressions require special release normalization. Rewrite those only in the next candidate canonical LaTeX source with native display math, preserving mathematical meanings and avoiding the old escaping ambiguity.

**Proposed sequence:** choose a reading route; map/territory/scale; hard vs soft dependencies; compact epistemic legend with EVIDENCE pointer; figures/source/claim boundaries; chapter entry checklist and limits.

**Disposition:** REVISION REQUIRED (structural duplication and formatting).

## OBJECTS — ATLAS-CH-OBJECTS-001

**Strength:** state/operator/flow/interface roles are clearly defined with a useful identical-matrix/different-semantic-role counterexample. The grammatical allegory gives its failure boundary, consistent with the editorial profile.

**ER-P1-06:** 19 numbered sections split four central definitions, category errors and handoffs too finely. Combine the four formal definitions, then develop a connected worked example and explicit failure boundaries.

**ER-P1-07:** the autonomous flow law should state the evolution parameter domain and distinguish a global semigroup/flow from a local flow defined only where composition makes sense; this is a mathematical precision clarification requiring technical check if changed.

**Proposed sequence:** role versus representation; precise four objects; worked category errors; state-dependent operators and interface contracts; consequences/handoffs.

**Disposition:** REVISION REQUIRED (structure and one technical-clarity item).

## EVIDENCE — ATLAS-CH-EVIDENCE-001

**Strength:** the claim/support packet separates epistemic class, support, scope, forbidden entailments and downstream permission. The exact parity example correctly distinguishes finite testing from a universal proof. The table explicitly denies a scalar ladder of evidence.

**ER-P1-08 (section 2):** K=(q,tau,S,Omega,N,D) and S-maps-to-q are written as untypeset prose notation. Typeset as semantic LaTeX math and distinguish the support relation from logical implication.

**ER-P1-09:** cut repeated map/legend definitions already introduced in MAP. Spend recovered space on a worked use of the packet across claim types and a practical reader exercise.

**Proposed sequence:** precise claim-support object; illustrative finite proof-versus-witness; evidence role table; interpretation and scope errors; source identity versus authority; downstream permissions.

**Disposition:** REVISION REQUIRED (typesetting and narrative integration).

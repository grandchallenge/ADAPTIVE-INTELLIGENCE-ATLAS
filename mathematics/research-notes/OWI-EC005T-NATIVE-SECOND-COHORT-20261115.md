**Qualification standard, locked before reading source:** A source family qualifies as a *natural mixed condition* candidate only if original actually selected public replies show both a requested **action-needed** and a genuinely **authorized no-action/fully satisfied** instance under the **same original public task's conditional rule**. Cases of two different actions, existing unrelated records, source lookup failures, or a target scheduled for another mutation **do not qualify**. A single originally conditional target may show one side only; it does not establish both natural states. An uncertain public tool response remains unknown. Qualifying conditions are a necessary substrate for future chosen-only training, not sufficient for demonstrating active observation value or outperforming strong source-public scripts. This predeclared full survey is a bounded finite audit, not proof the entire original corpus lacks natural clean cases.
